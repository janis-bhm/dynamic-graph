* (Splay) Top Tree
  a top tree $\mathbb{T}$ represents a forest of trees $F$.
  the leaves of the top tree are edges (labeled vertices) of the underlying tree $T\in F$.
  the internal nodes are clusters $C$, and are connected sets of edges in the underlying tree.
  the boundary vertices $\partial{C}$ of a cluster $C$ are vertices of $C$ that are either external boundary vertices of $T$ (exposed vertices), or vertices incident to an edge in $T\setminus{C}$,  or vertices with a label $l$ in $T\setminus{C}$:
  $\partial{C}=\{v \in C\cap{T} | v \in \partial{T} \wedge (\exists{e} \in T\setminus{C} \vee v \in e) \wedge (\exists{l} \in T\setminus{C} \vee l \in v)\}$

  Clusters can either be /path clusters/ if $|\partial{C}| = 2$ or /point clusters/ if $|\partial{C} < 2|$.
  The path of a path cluster $\pi{(C)}$ with boundary vertices $v, w$ is the path $v\cdots{w}$.
  The /central vertex/ of a cluster is the vertex shared by its children.
  Let $Ch(C)$ denote the children of $C$.
  The set of boundary vertices of a cluster is equal to the union of the boundary vertices in its children and the external boundary vertices of the clusters tree:
  $\boxed{
  \partial C
  =
  \bigcup_{C' \in \operatorname{Ch}_{\Tau}(C)} \partial C'
  \;\cup\;
  \bigl(\partial T \cap V(C)\bigr)
  }$

** CoverLevel
   $C$ is a cluster in $T$.
   Each cluster maintains a cover level $cover_C$, which is the minimal value of the cover levels of all path edges of $C$ and the maximum level of $T$:
   $cover_C=min \{c(e) | e \in \pi(C)\} \cup \{l_{\texttt{max}}\}$

   Each cluster maintains a cover level $globalcover_C$, which is the minimal value of the cover levels of all non-pathedges of $C$ and the maximum level of $T$:
   $globalcover_C = \texttt{min}\{c(e) | e \in C\setminus{\pi(C)}\} \cup \{l_{\texttt{max}}\}$

 Each cluster also maintains two edges, $minpathedge_C$, which is the path-edge with the minimal cover level or nil if no such edge exists,
 and $minglobaledge_C$, which is the non-path edge with the minimal cover level if C is not equal to its path (contains point clusters), or nil if the conditions are not met.

 Then, the =cover_level(v)= function returns $globalcover_C$ for $C=\texttt{Expose}(v)$,
 =cover_level(v,w)= returns $cover_C$ for $C = \texttt{Expose}(v,w)$
 =min_covered_edge(v)=   returns $minglobaledge_C$ for $C = \texttt{Expose}(v)$,
 and =min_covered_edge(v,w)= returns $minpathedge_C$ for $C = \texttt{Expose}(v,w)$
